## Open audit

- **September 2026 PF.** The product works September on one blended ceiling (15,000 × 16/30 + 25,000 × 14/30 = ₹19,667). Practitioners disagree: some split the month's wage by days against each ceiling (12% × (8,000 + 9,333) = ₹2,080 on a ₹20,000 wage, where the blended ceiling gives ₹2,360), and [Argus Partners](https://www.argus-p.com/papers-publications/thought-paper/revision-to-epf-wage-ceiling-coverage-contributions-and-the-september-transition/) reads the notification as giving no part-month ceiling at all. No EPFO clarification of the method was found as of 12 Nov 2026. Decide before relying on a September finding.
- **ESIC ceiling** is still ₹21,000 (₹25,000 for persons with disability) as of November 2026; no notification revising it was found.

- PT: all bundled states and local body schedules require official current schedule and supersession checks; there are 22 state entries in `pt_defaults.py`. City-specific taxes cannot be inferred from state names.
- LWF: other bundled states and statutory establishment/worker exclusions require official review; there are 15 state entries in `lwf_defaults.py`. The two corrected rates above do not prove all-state coverage.
- Minimum wages: there is no nationwide tenant rate seed. Appropriate-government schedules vary by employment, zone, skill and revision date. The [Chief Labour Commissioner minimum wages page](https://clc.gov.in/clc/min-wages) publishes central-sphere VDA orders; these cannot stand in for every state's schedules.
- The `slab_rules` PT/LWF table now accepts effective-from/effective-to dates and source references, and lookup selects the applicable version for the payroll period. Legacy undated rows still apply as fallback; importing defaults with overwrite or resetting can delete historical rows. Existing tenant imports are not automatically rewritten. The LWF validator now applies explicitly scheduled contribution months as full deductions; unverified states without month schedules still use monthly equivalents. Historical versions remain unavailable.
- Do not run `reset-defaults` or `import-defaults/all?overwrite=true` as a compliance update until each state and historical version has been checked. Existing tenant imports are not automatically changed by edits to bundled defaults.

Two LWF current-rate corrections and the Maharashtra and Karnataka due-month checks are implemented in this audit. They do not make the complete product ready for release.
